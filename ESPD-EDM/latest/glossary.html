Glossary

Aggregate Business Information Entities (ABIEs)
Root nodes and top-level structures of UBL documents. Children of document ABIEs may be Basic Business Information Entities (BBIEs) or Association Business Information Entities (ASBIEs), never ABIEs. All UBL document ABIEs (and only UBL document ABIEs) are defined within individual namespaces specific to each document as both elements and types. (https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.4/UBL-2.4.html#S-BUSINESS-INFORMATION-ENTITIES)

Association Business Information Entities (ASBIEs)
Substructures of a UBL document. Children of ASBIEs may be Basic Business Information Entities (BBIEs) or other ASBIEs, never Aggregate Business Information Entities (ABIEs). All UBL ASBIEs (and only UBL ASBIEs) are members as elements of the UBL common aggregate components namespace, denoted in UBL schemas by the cac: prefix. (https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.4/UBL-2.4.html#S-BUSINESS-INFORMATION-ENTITIES)

Basic Business Information Entities (BBIEs)
Leaf nodes of every UBL document structure. These are ordinary data fields such as one would expect to find in any business form, and they are realized in the schemas as individual XML elements at the bottom level of the document tree with simple content representing amounts, codes, quantities, and so on. All UBL BBIE elements (and only UBL BBIE elements) are members of the UBL common basic components namespace, conventionally denoted in UBL schemas by the cbc: prefix. (https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.4/UBL-2.4.html#S-BUSINESS-INFORMATION-ENTITIES)

Buyer (Contracting Authority)
The state, regional or local authority, body governed by public law, or association of such authorities or bodies, that runs a procurement procedure. In the ESPD the buyer defines the exclusion grounds and selection criteria, prepares the ESPD Request, and evaluates the ESPD Responses. "Buyer" is the standard term; "contracting authority" is the equivalent term used in Directive 2014/24/EU. See Actors and Roles.

Candidate
An economic operator that has sought an invitation, or has been invited, to take part in a restricted procedure, competitive procedure with negotiation, competitive dialogue or innovation partnership. Under Article 59(4) of Directive 2014/24/EU the buyer may request supporting documents from any candidate at any moment during the procedure.

Code lists
A code list is an artifact listing the codes relevant to specific terms, grouped by semantic meaning, e.g., a code list for country or language. See Code Lists.

code list document
A code list document has the root element <gc:CodeList>. It contains metadata describing the code list as a whole, as well as explicit code list data. (http://docs.oasis-open.org/codelist/approved/genericode/oasis-code-list-representation-genericode.html)

Context/value association (CVA)
File that specifies the relationship from information items found in different document contexts to one or more external genericode files for each item. (http://docs.oasis-open.org/codelist/ContextValueAssociation/doc/context-value-association.html)

Criteria, in the context of the ESPD, either selection or exclusion, are those applied to each economic operator on application to tender to validate qualification. Buyers stipulate the criteria, and economic operators provide evidence that they comply with them.

Components prefixed with cac: and cbc

  • cbc: common basic component is a leaf element / data node corresponding to the UBL specification Basic Business Information Entity (BBIE).

  • cac: common aggregate component is one that can contain more cac or cbc elements, corresponding to the UBL specification Aggregate Business Information Entity (ABIE).

eCertis
Information system that helps identify different certificates requested in procurement procedures across the EU. It is the authoritative source of ESPD criteria and their UUIDs. (https://ec.europa.eu/tools/ecertis/#/about)

Evidence
Documentation supporting an economic operator’s response to a criterion (for example a certificate, registration or reference). In the ESPD Response, evidence is referenced rather than embedded; a single piece of evidence may support more than one criterion. Under Article 59(4) of Directive 2014/24/EU the buyer may request supporting documents at any moment during the procedure, and the winning tenderer provides full documentary evidence before award.

Exclusion grounds
The situations, defined in Article 57 of Directive 2014/24/EU, in which an economic operator must or may be excluded from a procurement procedure (for example criminal convictions, unpaid taxes or social security contributions, or insolvency). Mandatory exclusion grounds cannot be removed by the buyer; discretionary ones may be selected or deselected. Exclusion grounds apply to the whole procedure, that is, to all lots. See Exclusion Grounds.

Economic Operator (EO)
Any natural or legal person, or public entity, or group of such persons or entities, including temporary associations, that offers works, supplies or services on the market within the context of a public procurement procedure. In the ESPD, the economic operator submits the ESPD Response as its self-declaration. Its roles include Sole Tenderer, Group Leader, Group Member, other entity relied upon, and Subcontractor. See Actors and Roles.

eForms
EU legislative open standard for publishing public procurement data. Standard forms used by public buyers to publish notices on Tenders Electronic Daily. (https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/public-procurement/digital-procurement/eforms_en)

European Skills, Competences, Qualifications and Occupations (ESCO)
European Commission project classifying professional occupations and skills relevant for the EU labour market. (https://ec.europa.eu/esco/portal)

EU Vocabularies
Multilingual, multidisciplinary thesaurus covering the activities of the EU. (https://op.europa.eu/en/web/eu-vocabularies)

European Single Procurement Document (ESPD)
The economic operator’s self-declaration, used as preliminary evidence in a public procurement procedure that the operator is not in a situation giving rise to exclusion and meets the selection criteria. Introduced in Article 59 of Directive 2014/24/EU and specified by Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/7. Strictly, "the ESPD" is the economic operator’s self-declaration, carried by the ESPD Response; the ESPD Request is the buyer’s specification and is not itself an ESPD. (https://github.com/OP-TED/ESPD-EDM)

European Single Procurement Document Exchange Data Model (ESPD-EDM)
Data model developed by the European Commission to define the exchange of ESPD documents between procurement stakeholders' systems. Version 5.0.0 is based on UBL 2.4 and defines the ESPD Request and ESPD Response documents. (https://github.com/OP-TED/ESPD-EDM)

ESPD Request
The document prepared by the buyer that expresses the exclusion grounds and selection criteria required for a procurement procedure. It enables the economic operator to produce its ESPD, but is not itself an ESPD. In the ESPD-EDM it is carried by the UBL 2.4 QualificationApplicationRequest document. The ESPD Request may be published alongside the Contract Notice; if it is not, the criteria are stated in the procurement documents. See Request and Response Concepts.

ESPD Response
The document prepared by the economic operator that carries the actual ESPD self-declaration: the answers to each exclusion ground and selection criterion, together with references to the evidence. It is "the ESPD" in the strict legal sense. In the ESPD-EDM it is carried by the UBL 2.4 QualificationApplicationResponse document and must include all criteria stated in the corresponding ESPD Request. See Request and Response Concepts.

genericode
Single model and XML format defined by OASIS that can encode a broad range of code list information. (http://docs.oasis-open.org/codelist/approved/genericode/oasis-code-list-representation-genericode.html)

The ICT Standards for Procurement prescribe the standards for EU interoperable solutions. (https://joinup.ec.europa.eu/collection/ict-standards-procurement)

The Joinup platform
The European Commission’s one-stop shop for interoperable, open, and free digital government ICT solutions. (https://joinup.ec.europa.eu)

Lot
A subdivision of a procurement procedure that can be tendered for and awarded separately. In the ESPD, forms are filled in per lot: exclusion grounds automatically apply to all lots, whereas selection criteria apply per lot and may differ between lots. An economic operator tendering for several lots submits one ESPD Response per lot. Lot identifiers follow the eForms format (for example LOT-0001). See Lots Overview.

[[Namespace Prefixes, Namespace Prefixes]] Namespace prefixes
Used in XML to avoid element name conflicts. See the W3C "Namespaces in XML" specification.

Organization for the Advancement of Structured Information Standards (OASIS)
Standards body offering standardization and de jure approval for reference in international policy and procurement. (https://www.oasis-open.org/org/)

Open User Community (OUC)
Group of users that participate or work with the ESPD-EDM.

The Publications Office of the European Union (OP)
Interinstitutional office whose task is to publish the publications of the institutions of the European Union. (https://op.europa.eu/en/home)

QualificationApplicationRequest
The UBL 2.4 document type used by the ESPD-EDM to carry the ESPD Request. (https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.4/UBL-2.4.html)

QualificationApplicationResponse
The UBL 2.4 document type used by the ESPD-EDM to carry the ESPD Response. (https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.4/UBL-2.4.html)

Schematron
Rule-based validation language for making assertions about the presence or absence of patterns in XML trees. Used together with a CVA file to enforce ESPD business rules and code-list constraints that XSD alone cannot express. (https://www.schematron.com/)

Selection criteria
The requirements, defined in Article 58 of Directive 2014/24/EU, used to assess an economic operator’s suitability to pursue the professional activity, economic and financial standing, and technical and professional ability. Selection criteria are linked to specific lots and may differ between lots. See Selection Criteria.

Tenders Electronic Daily (TED)
Online version of the 'Supplement to the Official Journal' of the EU, dedicated to European public procurement. Above-threshold Contract Notices are published on TED. (https://ted.europa.eu/)

Tenderer
An economic operator that has submitted a tender in a procurement procedure. Under Article 59(4) of Directive 2014/24/EU the buyer may request supporting documents from any tenderer at any moment during the procedure, and the winning tenderer must provide full documentary evidence before contract award.

Testbed
The Interoperability Testbed is an online tool for developers of ESPD Services that enables them to validate their XML ESPD Request and Response documents against schematron rules.

UBL (Universal Business Language)
Royalty-free library of standard XML business documents supporting digitization of commercial and logistical processes. ESPD-EDM v5.0.0 uses UBL 2.4. (https://www.oasis-open.org/committees/tc_home.php?wg_abbrev=ubl#overview)

Uniform Resource Identifier (URI)
A compact sequence of characters that identifies an abstract or physical resource. Defined by RFC 3986 (which obsoletes the earlier RFC 2396). (https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3986)

XML (Extensible Markup Language)
Simple, flexible text format derived from SGML, playing an important role in the exchange of data on the Web. (https://www.w3.org/XML/)

XSLT (Extensible Stylesheet Language Transformations)
Language for transforming XML documents into other XML documents or other formats. (https://www.w3.org/TR/xslt-30/)

ISO 15000-5:2014 (ebXML Core Components Specification)
The ebXML Core Components Specification, providing the data-typing foundation used by UBL. It supersedes the earlier ISO/TS 15000-5:2005 Technical Specification and builds on the UN/CEFACT Core Component Library. (https://www.iso.org/standard/61433.html)
